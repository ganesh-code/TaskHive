FAQ

Common questions

What happens after my free trial ends?
+
After your 14-day trial, you can choose any paid plan or continue with our permanently free tier — which includes limited features. You will never be automatically charged. No credit card is required to start.
Can I cancel anytime?
+
Yes, absolutely. TaskHive has a flexible, cancel-anytime policy. If you cancel, you keep access to your paid features until the end of your current billing period. No penalties, no hidden fees.
Can I switch or upgrade plans later?
+
Yes! You can upgrade, downgrade, or switch billing cycles at any time from your account settings. Upgrades take effect immediately, and we pro-rate any price differences automatically.
Is there a discount for nonprofits or startups?
+
Yes — we offer special pricing for nonprofits, early-stage startups (under $1M ARR), and educational institutions. Reach out to our team at hello@taskhive.io with details about your organization.
How does per-user pricing work for large teams?
+
On Basic and Pro plans, you pay per active user per month. Enterprise plans include volume discounts for teams of 50+. You can add or remove users at any time and your bill adjusts automatically.
